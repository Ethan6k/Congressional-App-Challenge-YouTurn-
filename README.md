# YouTurn

**See where your habits are taking you, and turn toward where you want to be.**

YouTurn is an iPhone app for anyone working toward a fitness goal. It asks about your body, habits, and goals, then shows two possible futures side by side: one where you keep your current habits, and one where you follow a personalized plan the app builds for you.

Created by Ethan Hong for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge.

## Features

- **Two futures:** AI-generated images of you (from your photo, or a matching fictional person) at 6 months to 10 years ahead, one for your current habits and one for following your plan. Each time frame keeps its own images.
- **Weight forecast:** a week-by-week simulation of where your weight is heading, with plain-English explanations of every number.
- **Nutrition plan:** daily calorie, protein, and fiber targets, plus prioritized tips based on your answers and the foods you eat.
- **Workout plan:** a weekly plan built from your goal, schedule, experience, and where you work out, with a flowchart showing how each answer shaped it. Check off exercises as you go.
- **Gallery:** every photo and generated image, labeled with the health data it was made from.
- **Saved on your phone:** your answers, photos, images, and progress stay on the device and are restored when you reopen the app. "Reset all data" erases everything.

## How to run

Requirements: a Mac with **Xcode 26.5 or later**, and an iPhone or simulator running **iOS 26.5 or later**.

1. Open `TestApp.xcodeproj` in Xcode. Xcode downloads the Firebase package automatically.
2. Choose an iPhone simulator (or your iPhone) and press **Run**.
3. Image generation needs Firebase:
   - `TestApp/GoogleService-Info.plist` connects the app to the Firebase project.
   - In debug builds the app uses Firebase App Check's debug provider. The first run prints a debug token in Xcode's console; add it in the Firebase console under **App Check → Manage debug tokens**.

Everything except image generation works without an internet connection.

## Privacy and security

YouTurn follows the [Take9](https://take9.org) pledge: think about what data the app collects, how it's protected, and how the design reduces risk.

**What the app collects:** age, sex, height, weight, habits, goals, optional foods, an optional photo, generated images, and workout check-offs. Your name is used only for greetings. No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

**How it's protected:**
- Everything is stored only on the phone, in the app's private storage, using iOS file protection (encrypted while the phone is locked).
- Nothing leaves the phone except when the user taps **Generate**. The first time, the app explains exactly what will be sent and asks permission.
- Only what's needed to draw the images is sent to Google's Gemini (via Firebase, over HTTPS): stats, habits, plan targets, forecasts, and the photo if added. The user's name and food list are never sent. Photos are re-encoded before sending, which removes hidden photo data such as location.
- Firebase App Check is built in, so with App Check enforcement turned on in the Firebase console, only the real app can make image requests and the Firebase key can't simply be copied and misused.
- Typed input is limited in length and only used for on-phone keyword matching. It's never run as code or sent anywhere.
- **Reset all data** permanently erases the profile, photos, images, and progress. Users can also delete individual images.

YouTurn is for general wellness and education and isn't medical advice.
